01 / Our story

Built around the way students learn.

Learning programming can be confusing when explanations, revision material, and practice are scattered across different places. Beginners may know what they want to learn, but still struggle to find a clear path from the first concept to confident practice.

Codify was created as a focused learning space that brings these important steps closer together. Instead of making learning feel complicated, the goal is to present topics in an organised way, explain ideas in approachable language, and make revision easier to return to.

Codify is an ongoing project shaped by a simple idea: useful learning tools should be clear, accessible, and designed with students in mind.

“One concept at a time. One practice session at a time. Keep getting better.”

02 / Our mission

Make technical learning easier to start — and easier to continue.

Our mission is to reduce the friction that gets in the way of learning: unclear starting points, scattered revision material, and too few opportunities to check understanding.

Start with clarity

Present learning topics in a structured order, so learners can understand where to begin and what to explore next.

Make revision practical

Keep concise notes close to the learning journey, making it simpler to revisit key concepts when needed.

Encourage active practice

Help learners move beyond reading by attempting assessments and using the results to guide further study.

04 / Platform features

Tools that support your learning routine.

Codify is designed to keep the learning experience focused, approachable, and easy to navigate across different screen sizes.

Your learning resources, organised in one place.

Move between lessons, revision notes, and assessments without having to treat each part of learning as a separate task. Start with a concept, review it when needed, and practise to check your understanding.

Global search

Clear lesson structure

Account and activity tools

Assessment and certificates
