01 · Introduction

One place to learn, revise and prove your skills.

Codify is a web-based learning management system for programming students. It organises lessons into chapters and topics, tracks progress per learner, assesses understanding through timed exams and issues certificates that anyone can verify.

02 · Problem statement

Learning to code is scattered and unstructured.

Beginners know what they want to learn but struggle to find a clear path from the first concept to confident practice.

01

No clear starting point

Tutorials, videos and notes live in different places, so learners do not know what to study first or next.

02

Revision is inconvenient

Revisiting key ideas before an exam means hunting through long lessons instead of concise, focused notes.

03

No way to prove learning

Self-study rarely includes trustworthy assessments or credentials that someone else can verify.

Codify joins learning, revision, assessment and certification in one guided experience.

12 · Assessment integrity

Exams you can trust.

Randomised for every attempt

30 random questions with shuffled options, started and graded through server-side functions.

Focus monitoring

Full-screen exam; leaving it triggers warnings, and the third ends the attempt.

Copy and paste blocked

Context menu, copy and paste are disabled during the exam.

Watermarked screens

Name, username and email appear on every screen, so leaked questions are traceable.

16 · Challenges & solutions

Problems we met, and how we solved them.

ChallengeKeeping exams honest in a browser
SolutionServer-side grading, shuffled questions, full-screen focus warnings and watermarks
ChallengeManaging 341 lessons across 7 courses
SolutionA JSON syllabus plus Markdown files per topic, rendered by shared course pages
ChallengeMaking certificates trustworthy
SolutionUnlock rules based on progress and exam result, plus public QR and ID verification
ChallengeOne consistent experience on phones and desktops
SolutionA shared dark theme, responsive layouts and a reusable navbar and footer
