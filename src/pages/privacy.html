Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Codify, we collect and store only the essential information needed to provide you with a secure and personalized learning environment:

  • Account Credentials: Your email address and secure encrypted password.
  • Profile Metadata: Your first name, surname, unique username, and selected avatar URL (stored securely within your Supabase Auth metadata).
  • Authentication Providers: Information provided when authenticating via third-party providers such as Google or GitHub (e.g., your display name and default profile picture).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect for the following operational purposes:

  • Authenticating your sessions and managing account security via OTP and password verification.
  • Displaying your chosen username and profile avatar across the collaborative workspace and community features.
  • Sending critical account notifications, password recovery links, and learning updates.

3. Data Security & Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase infrastructure with strict JSON metadata isolation. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Authentication

If you choose to sign up or log in using Google or GitHub, those third-party services authenticate your identity and share basic public profile details with us according to your authorization settings with them.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You maintain full control over your account metadata and can update your display name, username, or avatar preferences directly through your profile settings.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect platform enhancements or security protocols. Any changes will be posted directly on this page.
